# Code of Conduct

TeamBoard contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and make space for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and languages. Discuss technical disagreements using specific examples and evidence. Treat others' time and privacy with care.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted personal advances and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable in TeamBoard project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or take other proportionate action. They will handle reports with discretion and remove themselves from decisions where they have a conflict of interest.
